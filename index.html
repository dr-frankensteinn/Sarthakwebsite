About Sarthak Academy

Sarthak Academy is an abacus, Vedic maths and phonics institute in Vasai East, founded in 2011 by Sanjana Sukhesh Champanerkar. For over fifteen years we have helped more than 1800 children across Vasai-Virar become faster, more accurate and more confident with numbers and language.

Our classes are small and hands-on. Children learn on the abacus, move on to mental calculation, and take part in our annual abacus competition, where every participant is recognised. Parents from Vasai East, Vasai West, Nalasopara and Virar visit us at Shubhlaxmi Commercial Complex, opposite Vasant Nagari Ground.

The Sarthak Methodology

Whole Brain Development

Our Abacus program stimulates both the left and right hemispheres of the brain, enhancing creativity alongside logic.

Concentration Mastery

We train students to focus for extended periods. This discipline translates directly to better performance in school exams.

Academic Excellence

Removing the fear of numbers builds immense self-confidence. Our students consistently top their math classes.
